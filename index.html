    What We Do

    Research Agenda

    Our lab aims to merge mechanistic biophysical modeling with AI-driven representation learning to create scalable frameworks for modeling, simulating, and controlling biological systems.

    Biophysical & Mathematical Modeling

    Developing mathematical and computational models to uncover the physical principles governing the organization, dynamics, and function of complex biological systems.

    Learn more →

    Statistical Inference & Dynamical Systems

    Developing principled statistical inference and ML techniques to infer dynamics, mechanisms, and governing laws from noisy, sparse, and high-dimensional data.

    Learn more →

    Applications in Life Sciences & Medicine

    Applying these approaches across scales—from gene regulation and cell-state dynamics to cellular organization, tissue mechanics, and disease—to enable mechanistic discovery from biological data.
