Suryanarayana Maddu, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor · Department of Applied Mathematics & Statistics · Data Science & AI Institute

My research lies at the interface of biophysical modeling, statistical inference, and scientific machine learning, with the broader goal of building predictive and mechanistic models of biological processes.

Before joining JHU, I was a Research Fellow at Center for Computational Biology, Flatiron Institute and Harvard University. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Max Planck Institute of Molecular Cell Biology and Genetics in Dresden, Germany, and my Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from the National Institute of Technology Karnataka, India.
