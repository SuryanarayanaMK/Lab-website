Suryanarayana Maddu, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor · Department of Applied Mathematics & Statistics · Data Science & AI Institute

My research sits at the intersection of applied mathematics, machine learning, and computational biology — developing methods to understand complex biological systems from single cells to tissues.

Before joining JHU, I was a postdoctoral fellow at Harvard University. I received my Ph.D. from the Max Planck Institute of Molecular Cell Biology and Genetics in Dresden, Germany.
