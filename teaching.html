Fall 2026: EN.553.413/613 — Applied Statistics and Data Science. An applied course connecting probability to statistical practice, covering estimation and hypothesis testing, simple and multiple linear regression with inference and diagnostics, resampling methods (bootstrap, permutation tests), model selection and regularization, logistic regression and generalized linear models, ANOVA, multiple testing, and flexible regression (splines, generalized additive models) — culminating in a modern applied data analysis workflow, including reproducibility, missing data, and dimension reduction, explored throughout with real datasets.
